What is Mental Health?

Mental health refers to a person's emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It determines how individuals think, feel, and act when facing different situations in life. Mental health also affects how people manage stress, interact with others, and make choices.

Maintaining good mental health is just as important as maintaining physical health. It helps people lead productive lives, build strong relationships, and overcome challenges.

Factors That Affect Mental Health

Biological Factors

Genetics, brain chemistry, and hormonal changes can influence mental health.

Life Experiences

Stressful experiences such as academic pressure, financial issues, or loss.

Lifestyle Habits

Poor sleep, unhealthy diets, lack of exercise, and social media overuse.

Environmental Factors

Work environment, social surroundings, and academic stress.

Signs of Mental Health Problems

  • Persistent sadness or mood changes
  • Excessive worry or anxiety
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Loss of interest in activities
  • Sleeping too much or too little
  • Feeling overwhelmed or hopeless
