Importance of Self Care

Self-care refers to activities individuals perform to maintain their mental and emotional health. Small habits like resting, relaxing, or talking with friends can improve well-being.

Types of Self Care

Physical Self Care

Exercise, healthy diet, and adequate sleep.

Emotional Self Care

Journaling, expressing feelings, gratitude practice.

Social Self Care

Spending time with loved ones and building relationships.

Mental Self Care

Meditation, reading, and learning new skills.
